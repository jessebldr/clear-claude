`df -h` — shows every mounted filesystem with sizes in human-readable units (G, M).

Useful variants:

- `df -h /path` — just the filesystem holding that path
- `df -i` — inode usage, for when `df -h` says there's space free but writes still fail
- `du -sh *` — size of each item in the current directory, to find what's eating the space
- `du -h --max-depth=1 / | sort -h` — drill down from the root to find the big directory
